# A2 Unit 05: Weather and Seasons

## Learning Objectives
- Learn vocabulary for weather conditions
- Talk about the weather in different seasons
- Use the verb "faire" with weather expressions
- Use the verb "pleuvoir" (to rain)
- Use the verb "neiger" (to snow)
- Understand weather forecasts
- Talk about temperature
- Describe seasonal activities

## Dialogue: Talking About the Weather

**Context**: Sophie and Marie are talking about the weather in Lyon.

---

**Sophie**: Marie, il fait quel temps aujourd'hui?
**Marie**: Il fait beau! Le soleil brille et il n'y a pas de nuages.

**Sophie**: Super! On peut aller se promener au parc.
**Marie**: Oui, c'est une bonne idée. Il fait environ 20 degrés.

**Sophie**: Parfait! Et demain, il va faire quel temps?
**Marie**: Demain, il va pleuvoir, je crois.

**Sophie**: Oh, non! Et après-demain?
**Marie**: Après-demain, il va faire froid et il va y avoir du vent.

**Sophie**: Ce n'est pas très agréable. Et cet été, il a fait quel temps?
**Marie**: Cet été, il a fait très chaud. Il a fait jusqu'à 40 degrés!

**Sophie**: 40 degrés? C'est très chaud! Et cet hiver, il a neigé?
**Marie**: Oui, il a neigé quelques fois. C'était très joli avec la neige.

**Sophie**: J'adore la neige! On peut faire du ski.
**Marie**: Moi aussi! J'adore l'hiver.

## Dialogue: Weather Forecast

**Context**: Sophie and Marie are watching the weather forecast on TV.

---

**Présentateur**: Bonsoir! Voici la météo pour demain.
**Marie**: Chut! J'écoute la météo.

**Présentateur**: À Lyon, il va faire nuageux avec des averses dans l'après-midi.
**Sophie**: Oh, non! Il va pleuvoir?

**Présentateur**: Oui, il va pleuvoir entre 14 heures et 16 heures.
**Marie**: On ne pourra pas aller au parc, alors.

**Présentateur**: Les températures seront entre 15 et 18 degrés.
**Sophie**: Il va faire frais.

**Présentateur**: Il y aura aussi du vent, surtout le matin.
**Marie**: Parfait pour les cerfs-volants!

**Présentateur**: À Paris, il va faire soleil avec des températures autour de 20 degrés.
**Sophie**: C'est mieux qu'à Lyon!

**Présentateur**: Et dans le sud, il va faire très chaud, jusqu'à 30 degrés.
**Marie**: J'aimerais être dans le sud!

## Grammar: The Verb "Faire" with Weather

### "Faire" for Weather

In French, the verb "faire" (to do/make) is often used to describe weather conditions:

| French | English | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| il fait beau | it's nice weather | /eel fay boh/ |
| il fait mauvais | it's bad weather | /eel fay moh-vay/ |
| il fait soleil | it's sunny | /eel fay soh-lay/ |
| il fait chaud | it's hot | /eel fay shoh/ |
| il fait frais | it's cool | /eel fay fresh/ |
| il fait froid | it's cold | /eel fay frwah/ |
| il fait du vent | it's windy | /eel fay doo von/ |
| il fait nuageux | it's cloudy | /eel fay noo-azh-uh/ |
| il fait gris | it's overcast | /eel fay gree/ |
| il fait du brouillard | it's foggy | /eel fay doo broo-yar/ |

**Examples:**
- Aujourd'hui, **il fait** beau. (Today, it's nice weather.)
- Demain, **il va faire** soleil. (Tomorrow, it's going to be sunny.)
- Hier, **il a fait** froid. (Yesterday, it was cold.)

### Important Notes:

1. **Subject**: Weather expressions with "faire" always use "il" (it) as the subject.
   - **Il** fait beau. (It's nice weather.)
   - **Il** fait chaud. (It's hot.)

2. **Tenses**: "Faire" can be used in different tenses to describe weather in different time periods.
   - Present: **Il fait** beau. (It's nice weather.)
   - Near Future: **Il va faire** soleil. (It's going to be sunny.)
   - Passé Composé: **Il a fait** froid. (It was cold.)

## Grammar: The Verb "Pleuvoir" (To Rain)

### Present Tense of "Pleuvoir"

"Pleuvoir" is an impersonal verb, meaning it doesn't have a subject in the traditional sense. It always uses "il" (it).

| Subject | French | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| - | il pleut | /eel pluh/ |

**Examples:**
- **Il pleut**. (It's raining.)
- **Il pleuvait**. (It was raining.)
- **Il va pleuvoir**. (It's going to rain.)
- **Il a plu**. (It rained.)

### Important Notes:

1. **Impersonal Verb**: "Pleuvoir" is always used with "il" and does not take a subject.
   - **Il pleut** (not "Je pleut" or "Nous pleuvons")

2. **Tenses**:
   - Present: **Il pleut**. (It's raining.)
   - Imperfect: **Il pleuvait**. (It was raining.)
   - Near Future: **Il va pleuvoir**. (It's going to rain.)
   - Passé Composé: **Il a plu**. (It rained.)

3. **Negative Form**: "ne...pas" surrounds "pleut"
   - **Il ne pleut pas**. (It's not raining.)

4. **Question Form**: Invert "il" and "pleut"
   - **Pleut-il**? (Is it raining?)

## Grammar: The Verb "Neiger" (To Snow)

### Present Tense of "Neiger"

Like "pleuvoir", "neiger" is also an impersonal verb and always uses "il".

| Subject | French | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| - | il neige | /eel nehzh/ |

**Examples:**
- **Il neige**. (It's snowing.)
- **Il neigeait**. (It was snowing.)
- **Il va neiger**. (It's going to snow.)
- **Il a neigé**. (It snowed.)

### Important Notes:

1. **Impersonal Verb**: "Neiger" is always used with "il" and does not take a subject.
   - **Il neige** (not "Je neige" or "Nous neigeons")

2. **Tenses**:
   - Present: **Il neige**. (It's snowing.)
   - Imperfect: **Il neigeait**. (It was snowing.)
   - Near Future: **Il va neiger**. (It's going to snow.)
   - Passé Composé: **Il a neigé**. (It snowed.)

3. **Negative Form**: "ne...pas" surrounds "neige"
   - **Il ne neige pas**. (It's not snowing.)

4. **Question Form**: Invert "il" and "neige"
   - **Neige-t-il**? (Is it snowing?)

## Grammar: Talking About Temperature

### Basic Temperature Expressions

| French | English | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Il fait... degrés. | It's... degrees. | /eel fay... day-gray/ |
| Il fait zéro degré. | It's zero degrees. | /eel fay zay-roh day-gray/ |
| Il fait 10 degrés. | It's 10 degrees. | /eel fay diz day-gray/ |
| Il fait 20 degrés. | It's 20 degrees. | /eel fay vahn day-gray/ |
| Il fait moins 5 degrés. | It's minus 5 degrees. | /eel fay mwan sank day-gray/ |

**Examples:**
- **Il fait** 25 degrés aujourd'hui. (It's 25 degrees today.)
- **Il va faire** 30 degrés demain. (It's going to be 30 degrees tomorrow.)
- **Il a fait** moins 10 degrés hier. (It was minus 10 degrees yesterday.)

### Temperature Ranges

| French | English | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| entre... et... | between... and... | /on-treh... ay/ |
| de... à... | from... to... | /duh... a/ |
| jusqu'à... | up to... | /zhoos-ka.../ |
| au-dessus de... | above... | /oh-deh-soo duh/ |
| en dessous de... | below... | /on deh-soo duh/ |

**Examples:**
- Les températures seront **entre** 15 **et** 20 degrés. (The temperatures will be between 15 and 20 degrees.)
- Il fera **de** 10 **à** 15 degrés. (It will be from 10 to 15 degrees.)
- Il fera **jusqu'à** 30 degrés. (It will be up to 30 degrees.)
- Il fera **au-dessus de** 25 degrés. (It will be above 25 degrees.)
- Il fera **en dessous de** zéro. (It will be below zero.)

### Asking About Temperature

| French | English | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Il fait combien? | How much is it? (temperature) | /eel fay kom-bee-en/ |
| Quelle est la température? | What's the temperature? | /kel ay la ton-peh-rah-toor/ |
| Combien de degrés? | How many degrees? | /kom-bee-en duh day-gray/ |
| Il fait chaud/froid? | Is it hot/cold? | /eel fay shoh/frwah/ |

**Examples:**
- **Il fait combien** aujourd'hui? (How much is it today? - temperature)
- **Quelle est la température**? (What's the temperature?)
- **Combien de degrés** fait-il? (How many degrees is it?)

### Seasonal Expressions

| French | English | Pronunciation |

| cet hiver | this winter | /set ee-vehr/ |
| l'hiver dernier | last winter | /lee-vehr dehr-nyay/ |

**Examples:**
- **En printemps**, il fait doux. (In spring, it's mild.)
- **En été**, il fait très chaud. (In summer, it's very hot.)
- **En automne**, il pleut souvent. (In autumn, it often rains.)
- **En hiver**, il fait froid. (In winter, it's cold.)

### Weather by Season

| Season | Weather | Activities |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Printemps** | il fait doux, il pleut parfois, il y a du soleil | faire des pique-niques, faire du vélo, cueillir des fleurs |
| **Été** | il fait chaud/très chaud, il fait soleil, il y a des orages | se baigner, bronzer, faire des voyages, aller à la plage |
| **Automne** | il fait frais, il pleut souvent, il y a du vent, il y a du brouillard | faire de la randonnée, ramasser des feuilles, faire des promenades |
| **Hiver** | il fait froid/très froid, il neige parfois, il y a du gel | faire du ski, faire du snowboard, faire de la luge, faire un bonhomme de neige |

**Examples:**
- **Au printemps**, il pleut parfois. On peut faire des pique-niques. (In spring, it sometimes rains. We can have picnics.)
- **En été**, il fait très chaud. On peut aller à la plage. (In summer, it's very hot. We can go to the beach.)
- **En automne**, il y a du brouillard. On peut faire de la randonnée. (In autumn, there's fog. We can go hiking.)
- **En hiver**, il neige parfois. On peut faire du ski. (In winter, it sometimes snows. We can go skiing.)

## Cultural Note: Weather in France

France has a diverse climate with different weather patterns in different regions. Here are some insights into weather in France:

**Regional Climates**:
- **Paris and Île-de-France**: Oceanic climate with mild winters and warm summers. Rain is distributed throughout the year.
  - Winter: 0-10°C (32-50°F)
  - Summer: 15-25°C (59-77°F)
  - Rain: Throughout the year

- **North (Lille, Normandy)**: Oceanic climate with cool summers and mild winters. Frequent rain.
  - Winter: 0-8°C (32-46°F)
  - Summer: 15-22°C (59-72°F)
  - Rain: Very frequent

- **Southwest (Bordeaux, Toulouse)**: Oceanic climate with warm summers and mild winters. Less rain than the north.
  - Winter: 2-10°C (36-50°F)
  - Summer: 20-30°C (68-86°F)
  - Rain: Moderate

- **Southeast (Marseille, Nice)**: Mediterranean climate with hot, dry summers and mild winters.
  - Winter: 5-12°C (41-54°F)
  - Summer: 25-35°C (77-95°F)
  - Rain: Rare in summer, more frequent in winter

- **East (Strasbourg, Lyon)**: Continental climate with cold winters and hot summers.
  - Winter: -5 to 5°C (23-41°F)
  - Summer: 20-35°C (68-95°F)
  - Rain: Moderate

- **Mountains (Alps, Pyrenees)**: Mountain climate with cold winters and cool summers. Snow in winter.
  - Winter: -10 to 0°C (14-32°F)
  - Summer: 10-20°C (50-68°F)
  - Snow: Frequent in winter

**Seasonal Highlights**:
- **Spring (Printemps)**: March to May. Spring is a beautiful time in France with blooming flowers and mild temperatures. It's a great time for outdoor activities and sightseeing.
- **Summer (Été)**: June to August. Summer is hot and sunny, especially in the south. It's the peak tourist season with many festivals and events.
- **Autumn (Automne)**: September to November. Autumn brings cooler temperatures and beautiful fall foliage, especially in the countryside.
- **Winter (Hiver)**: December to February. Winter can be cold, especially in the north and mountains. It's a great time for winter sports in the Alps and Pyrenees.

**Weather Forecasts**:
- Weather forecasts (météo) are very popular in France and are broadcast frequently on TV and radio.
- **Météo-France** is the national weather service and provides accurate forecasts.
- Weather forecasts often include:
  - Temperature (température)
  - Weather conditions (temps)
  - Wind (vent)
  - Humidity (humidité)
  - UV index (indice UV)
  - Pollen count (indice pollinique)

**Weather and Daily Life**:
- The French often plan their activities around the weather.
- **Bonne météo**: Good weather is an opportunity for outdoor activities like picnics, walks, and sports.
- **Mauvaise météo**: Bad weather is a time for indoor activities like reading, cooking, or visiting museums.
- The French often discuss the weather as a way to start conversations.

**Weather and Fashion**:
- The French are known for their style, and weather plays a big role in what they wear.
- **Spring**: Light jackets, layers, and colorful clothing as the weather warms up.
- **Summer**: Light, breathable fabrics, dresses, shorts, and sandals. Sunglasses and hats are common.
- **Autumn**: Layers, scarves, and light jackets as the weather cools down.
- **Winter**: Warm coats, scarves, gloves, and boots. In the mountains, ski clothing is common.

**Weather and Food**:
- Weather influences French cuisine and eating habits.
- **Summer**: Light meals, salads, cold soups (like gazpacho), and refreshing drinks.
- **Winter**: Hearty stews, soups, and warm drinks like hot chocolate and mulled wine (vin chaud).
- **Spring/Autumn**: A mix of light and hearty dishes, often featuring seasonal produce.

**Weather and Travel**:
- Weather can greatly affect travel plans in France.
- **Summer**: Peak travel season with many tourists. Book accommodations and transportation in advance.
- **Winter**: Ski resorts in the Alps and Pyrenees are very popular. Book early for the best prices.
- **Spring/Autumn**: Shoulder seasons with fewer tourists and pleasant weather for sightseeing.
- **Rain**: Always carry an umbrella (parapluie) or raincoat (imperméable), especially in the north.

**Weather and Agriculture**:
- France is a major agricultural producer, and weather plays a crucial role in farming.
- **Vineyards**: Weather affects grape growth and wine production. A good year (un bon millésime) is one with ideal weather conditions.
- **Wheat**: France is a major wheat producer, and weather affects the harvest.
- **Fruits and Vegetables**: Seasonal produce is highly valued in French cuisine.

**Weather and Holidays**:
- Many French holidays and festivals are tied to seasons and weather:
  - **Fête de la Musique** (June 21): Celebrated in summer with outdoor concerts.
  - **Bastille Day** (July 14): Celebrated with fireworks and outdoor events.
  - **Noël** (Christmas): Celebrated in winter with decorations and markets.
  - **Pâques** (Easter): Celebrated in spring with egg hunts and family gatherings.

**Weather Proverbs**:
- The French have many proverbs related to weather:
  - **"Avril fait la fleur, mai en a l'honneur"** (April makes the flower, May has the honor) - April showers bring May flowers.
  - **"À la Sainte-Catherine, tout bois prend racine"** (By St. Catherine's Day, all wood takes root) - A gardening proverb.
  - **"Quand le chat est dehors, il pleut ou il vente"** (When the cat is outside, it's raining or windy) - Similar to "It's raining cats and dogs."
  - **"Petite pluie abat grand vent"** (A light rain calms a strong wind) - Similar to "After the storm comes the calm."

**Weather Superstitions**:
- Some French people believe in weather-related superstitions:
  - **"Si le ciel est rouge le soir, il fera beau le lendemain. Si le ciel est rouge le matin, il pleuvra dans la journée"** (If the sky is red in the evening, it will be nice the next day. If the sky is red in the morning, it will rain during the day.)
  - **"Quand les hirondelles volent bas, il va pleuvoir"** (When swallows fly low, it's going to rain.)
  - **"Si tu entends le coucou chanter en été, il pleuvra dans les sept jours"** (If you hear the cuckoo sing in summer, it will rain within seven days.)
