# B1 Unit 10: Future and Technology

## Learning Objectives
- Discuss future plans and predictions using appropriate future tenses
- Talk about technology and its impact on society
- Use the future perfect tense (futur antérieur) for completed future actions
- Use the future in the past (futur dans le passé) for reported future actions
- Discuss the benefits and drawbacks of technological advancements
- Understand and use future and technology vocabulary in context
- Express opinions about future technological developments
- Make predictions about how technology will change our lives

## Dialogue: Planning for the Future

**Context**: Sophie and her colleague Marc are discussing their future career plans and how technology might affect them.

---

**Sophie**: Marc, qu'est-ce que tu feras dans cinq ans?
**Marc**: Oh, c'est une bonne question! J'aurai probablement fini mes études et je travaillerai dans le domaine de l'intelligence artificielle.

**Sophie**: Ça a l'air passionnant! Et qu'est-ce que tu penses faire après?
**Marc**: Je pense que je continuerai mes études pour obtenir un doctorat. Et toi?

**Sophie**: Moi, j'aurai peut-être mon propre entreprise d'ici là. Je veux créer une application pour aider les gens à apprendre les langues.
**Marc**: C'est une excellente idée! Avec la technologie actuelle, ce sera probablement plus facile que jamais.

**Sophie**: Oui, et d'ici cinq ans, la technologie aura encore évolué. On ne sait pas ce qui sera possible!
**Marc**: C'est vrai. Je me demande quels nouveaux métiers existeront dans le futur.

**Sophie**: C'est difficile à prédire, mais je suis sûre qu'il y aura beaucoup d'opportunités dans le domaine de la technologie.
**Marc**: Oui, et il faudra que les gens s'adaptent à ces changements.

## Dialogue: Discussing Technological Advances

**Context**: Amélie and Thomas are talking about recent technological developments and their impact on daily life.

---

**Amélie**: Thomas, tu as vu les dernières avancées en matière de voitures autonomes?
**Thomas**: Oui, c'est incroyable! Je pense que dans dix ans, la plupart des voitures seront autonomes.

**Amélie**: Tu crois que ça changera beaucoup de choses?
**Thomas**: Absolument! Par exemple, il y aura probablement moins d'accidents de la route.

**Amélie**: C'est vrai. Et aussi, les gens pourront faire d'autres choses pendant leurs trajets.
**Thomas**: Exactement! Et ça réduira aussi la pollution, si les voitures autonomes sont électriques.

**Amélie**: Mais est-ce que tu penses qu'il y aura des problèmes?
**Thomas**: Bien sûr, il y aura des défis. Par exemple, qu'est-ce qui se passera si une voiture autonome a un accident? Qui sera responsable?

**Amélie**: C'est une bonne question. Et aussi, est-ce que tout le monde pourra se payer une voiture autonome?
**Thomas**: Ce sont des questions importantes. Mais je pense que globalement, ça améliorera notre qualité de vie.

## Dialogue: Interview with a Tech Entrepreneur

**Context**: A journalist interviews Madame Lefèvre, the founder of a successful tech startup.

---

**Journaliste**: Madame Lefèvre, pouvez-vous nous parler de votre entreprise?
**Madame Lefèvre**: Bien sûr! J'ai fondé cette entreprise il y a cinq ans. Nous développons des applications pour aider les entreprises à devenir plus efficaces.

**Journaliste**: Quels sont vos objectifs pour l'avenir?
**Madame Lefèvre**: D'ici trois ans, nous aurons lancé au moins cinq nouvelles applications. Nous espérons aussi nous étendre à l'international.

**Journaliste**: Comment est-ce que vous voyez l'avenir de la technologie?
**Madame Lefèvre**: Je pense que la technologie continuera à évoluer rapidement. D'ici dix ans, nous aurons probablement des technologies que nous ne pouvons même pas imaginer aujourd'hui.

**Journaliste**: Est-ce que vous pensez que la technologie remplacera certains métiers?
**Madame Lefèvre**: Oui, certains métiers disparaîtront, mais de nouveaux métiers apparaîtront aussi. Il faudra que les gens soient prêts à s'adapter.

**Journaliste**: Quel conseil donneriez-vous aux jeunes qui veulent travailler dans la technologie?
**Madame Lefèvre**: Je leur conseillerais d'être toujours prêts à apprendre de nouvelles choses. La technologie change constamment, donc il faut être flexible.

**Journaliste**: Merci, Madame Lefèvre, pour cet entretien.
**Madame Lefèvre**: Je vous en prie. Ce fut un plaisir.

## Grammar: Future Perfect Tense (Futur Antérieur)

The future perfect tense (futur antérieur) is used to express an action that will have been completed before another action in the future. It's the future equivalent of the pluperfect tense.

### Formation

The future perfect is formed with the future simple of the auxiliary verb (avoir or être) + the past participle of the main verb.

- For verbs using **avoir**: j'aurai + past participle
- For verbs using **être**: je serai + past participle

### Conjugation of Avoir in Future Simple

| Pronoun | Avoir (future simple) |
|---------|------------------------|
| je | j'aurai |
| tu | tu auras |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on aura |
| nous | nous aurons |
| vous | vous aurez |
| ils/elles | ils/elles auront |

### Conjugation of Être in Future Simple

| Pronoun | Être (future simple) |
|---------|----------------------|
| je | je serai |
| tu | tu seras |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on sera |
| nous | nous serons |
| vous | vous serez |
| ils/elles | ils/elles seront |

### Examples with Avoir

| Pronoun | Future Perfect (avoir) | Example |
|---------|------------------------|---------|
| je | j'aurai + past participle | J'aurai **fini** mes devoirs avant 8h. (I will have finished my homework before 8pm.) |
| tu | tu auras + past participle | Tu auras **mangé** avant qu'il n'arrive. (You will have eaten before he arrives.) |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on aura + past participle | Il aura **lu** le livre avant la fin de la semaine. (He will have read the book before the end of the week.) |
| nous | nous aurons + past participle | Nous aurons **parti** avant qu'il ne pleuve. (We will have left before it rains.) |
| vous | vous aurez + past participle | Vous aurez **reçu** la lettre avant demain. (You will have received the letter before tomorrow.) |
| ils/elles | ils/elles auront + past participle | Ils auront **terminé** avant que nous arrivions. (They will have finished before we arrive.) |

### Examples with Être

| Pronoun | Future Perfect (être) | Example |
|---------|-----------------------|---------|
| je | je serai + past participle | Je serai **parti(e)** avant 10h. (I will have left before 10am.) |
| tu | tu seras + past participle | Tu seras **arrivé(e)** avant qu'il ne parte. (You will have arrived before he leaves.) |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on sera + past participle | Il sera **tombé** avant que nous arrivions. (He will have fallen before we arrive.) |
| nous | nous serons + past participle | Nous serons **rentrés** avant minuit. (We will have returned before midnight.) |
| vous | vous serez + past participle | Vous serez **sorti(e)(s)** avant qu'il ne fasse nuit. (You will have gone out before nightfall.) |
| ils/elles | ils/elles seront + past participle | Ils seront **allés** avant que le magasin ne ferme. (They will have gone before the store closes.) |

### Use Cases

1. **Action completed before another future action:**
   - Quand tu **arriveras**, j'**aurai déjà fini** mon travail. (When you arrive, I will have already finished my work.)
   - Elle **sera partie** avant que tu ne reviennes. (She will have left before you come back.)

2. **In time clauses:**
   - **Avant que** + present subjunctive: Je partirai avant que tu **ne reviennes**. (I will leave before you come back.)
   - **Après que** + future perfect: Je partirai après que tu **seras revenu**. (I will leave after you have come back.)
   - **Quand** / **Lorsque**: Quand il **aura fini**, il viendra nous voir. (When he will have finished, he will come to see us.)

3. **With expressions like:**
   - **d'ici + time period**: D'ici la fin de l'année, j'**aurai économisé** assez d'argent. (By the end of the year, I will have saved enough money.)
   - **dans + time period**: Dans cinq ans, elle **aura obtenu** son diplôme. (In five years, she will have obtained her degree.)

## Grammar: Future in the Past (Futur dans le Passé)

The future in the past (futur dans le passé) is used to express a future action from a past perspective. It's the equivalent of the future simple but used in reported speech or narratives set in the past.

### Formation

The future in the past is formed with the imperfect of the auxiliary verb (avoir or être) + the infinitive of the main verb.

- For most verbs: j'allais + infinitive (using aller in the imperfect)
- For verbs that use être in compound tenses: j'étais + infinitive (less common)

However, the most common way to express future in the past is to use the **conditional present** (which looks like the future simple but with different endings).

### Conditional Present Formation

The conditional present is formed by taking the future simple stem of the verb and adding the imperfect endings.

For regular verbs:
- **-er verbs**: infinitive stem + conditional endings
- **-ir verbs**: infinitive stem + conditional endings
- **-re verbs**: infinitive stem + conditional endings

**Conditional Endings:**
| Pronoun | Ending |
|---------|--------|
| je | -ais |
| tu | -ais |
| il/elle/on | -ait |
| nous | -ions |
| vous | -iez |
| ils/elles | -aient |

### Examples

**Regular -er verb (parler):**
| Pronoun | Conditional Present |
|---------|----------------------|
| je | je parlerais |
| tu | tu parlerais |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on parlerait |
| nous | nous parlerions |
| vous | vous parleriez |
| ils/elles | ils/elles parleraient |

**Regular -ir verb (finir):**
| Pronoun | Conditional Present |
|---------|----------------------|
| je | je finirais |
| tu | tu finirais |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on finirait |
| nous | nous finirions |
| vous | vous finiriez |
| ils/elles | ils/elles finiraient |

**Regular -re verb (vendre):**
| Pronoun | Conditional Present |
|---------|----------------------|
| je | je vendrais |
| tu | tu vendrais |
| il/elle/on | il/elle/on vendrait |
| nous | nous vendrions |
| vous | vous vendriez |
| ils/elles | ils/elles vendraient |

### Irregular Verbs in Conditional Present

| Infinitive | Future Stem | Conditional Present |
|------------|--------------|----------------------|
| avoir | aur- | j'aurais |
| être | ser- | je serais |
| aller | ir- | j'irais |
| faire | fer- | je ferais |
| pouvoir | pourr- | je pourrais |
| vouloir | voudr- | je voudrais |
| savoir | saur- | je saurais |
| devoir | devr- | je devrais |
| recevoir | recevr- | je recevrais |
| venir | viendr- | je viendrais |
| tenir | tiendr- | je tiendrais |

### Use Cases for Future in the Past

1. **Reported speech:**
   - Direct: "Je partirai demain." (I will leave tomorrow.)
   - Indirect: Il a dit qu'il **partirait** le lendemain. (He said that he would leave the next day.)

2. **Narratives set in the past:**
   - Je savais qu'il **viendrait** me voir. (I knew that he would come to see me.)
   - Elle a promis qu'elle **ferait** de son mieux. (She promised that she would do her best.)

3. **Expressing hypothetical situations:**
   - Si j'avais de l'argent, j'**achèterais** une nouvelle voiture. (If I had money, I would buy a new car.)
   - Il **serait** plus heureux s'il avait plus de temps libre. (He would be happier if he had more free time.)

4. **Polite requests:**
   - Je **voudrais** un café, s'il vous plaît. (I would like a coffee, please.)
   - **Pourrais**-tu m'aider? (Could you help me?)

### Comparison with Future Simple

| Tense | Use | Example |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Future Simple | Expresses future actions from a present perspective | Je partirai demain. (I will leave tomorrow.) |
| Conditional Present (Future in the Past) | Expresses future actions from a past perspective or hypothetical situations | Il a dit qu'il partirait demain. (He said he would leave tomorrow.) |

## Cultural Note: Technology and Innovation in France

France has a **long history of technological innovation** and continues to be at the **forefront of technological development** in many fields. The country has produced numerous inventors, scientists, and engineers who have made significant contributions to the world.

### Historical Technological Achievements

**Early Inventions:**
- The **cinematograph** was invented by the Lumière brothers in 1895, making France the birthplace of cinema.
- **Photography** was developed by Nicéphore Niépce and Louis Daguerre in the early 19th century.
- The **stethoscope** was invented by René Laënnec in 1816, revolutionizing medical diagnosis.
- The **bicycle** (or velocipede) was developed in France in the 1860s.

**20th Century Innovations:**
- **Radioactivity** was discovered by Henri Becquerel in 1896, with Marie and Pierre Curie making significant contributions to its study.
- The **Concorde**, the world's first supersonic passenger airliner, was developed jointly by France and the UK.
- The **TGV** (Train à Grande Vitesse), France's high-speed train, was introduced in 1981 and remains one of the fastest trains in the world.
- The **smart card** (carte à puce) was invented in France and is now used worldwide for various applications.
- **Minitel**, an early online service, was launched in France in 1982, predating the widespread use of the Internet.

**Modern Innovations:**
- France is a leader in **nuclear energy**, with about 70% of its electricity coming from nuclear power plants.
- The country has developed advanced **aerospace technology**, with Airbus being a major global player.
- France is at the forefront of **renewable energy** development, particularly in solar and wind power.
- French companies are leaders in **luxury goods technology**, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern innovation.
- France has a strong **pharmaceutical industry**, with companies like Sanofi being global leaders.

### Technology in French Society

**Digital Transformation:**
France has been actively pursuing digital transformation across all sectors. The government has launched several initiatives to promote digital inclusion and innovation.

**Education:** French schools have been integrating technology into education, with programs to provide students with digital devices and training.

**Healthcare:** France has a sophisticated healthcare system that incorporates advanced medical technologies. Telemedicine and digital health records are becoming increasingly common.

**Transportation:** France has one of the most advanced transportation systems in the world. The TGV network connects major cities, and Paris has an extensive metro system. Electric vehicles and bike-sharing programs are also popular.

**Smart Cities:** Several French cities are implementing smart city technologies to improve urban life. Paris, Lyon, and Bordeaux are among the leaders in using data and technology to optimize city services.

### French Tech Ecosystem

**Startup Nation:** France has been actively promoting itself as a startup nation. The government has implemented policies to support entrepreneurship and innovation.

**Tech Hubs:** Paris is becoming a major European tech hub, with many startups and international tech companies establishing offices there. Other cities like Lyon, Toulouse, and Grenoble also have thriving tech scenes.

**Station F:** Located in Paris, Station F is one of the world's largest startup campuses. It provides a space for entrepreneurs to develop their ideas and connect with investors and mentors.

**Viva Technology:** This is a major annual technology conference held in Paris that brings together startups, investors, and tech leaders from around the world.

**France Digitale:** This is an association of French digital entrepreneurs that promotes the interests of the French tech ecosystem.

### Technology and French Culture

**Language Technology:** France has been active in developing technologies to support the French language. This includes speech recognition, natural language processing, and digital content in French.

**Cultural Preservation:** Technology is being used to preserve French cultural heritage. Digital archives, 3D scanning of historical sites, and virtual reality experiences are helping to make French culture more accessible.

**Media and Entertainment:** France has a strong media and entertainment industry that has embraced digital technology. French films, music, and games are widely distributed through digital platforms.

**Challenges:** While France has embraced technology, there are also challenges. These include ensuring digital inclusion for all citizens, addressing privacy concerns, and maintaining a balance between technological advancement and preserving traditional French values.

### The Future of Technology in France

Looking ahead, France is positioned to continue being a leader in technological innovation. The country's strong educational system, government support for research and development, and vibrant tech ecosystem provide a solid foundation for future advancements.

Key areas for future development include:
- **Artificial Intelligence:** France aims to become a leader in AI research and application.
- **Green Technology:** With a focus on sustainability, France is investing in green technologies.
- **Health Technology:** Advances in medical technology and biotechnology are expected.
- **Space Exploration:** France, through its space agency CNES, continues to be active in space exploration.
- **Quantum Computing:** France is investing in quantum computing research.

Understanding the role of technology in French society provides valuable insight into modern French culture and its approach to innovation and progress.
