# B1 Unit 02: Environment and Nature

## Learning Objectives
- Discuss environmental issues and concerns
- Describe natural landscapes and ecosystems
- Use the future tense to talk about environmental actions
- Use the conditional mood to express hypothetical situations
- Talk about weather patterns and climate
- Discuss conservation efforts and sustainable practices
- Understand and use environmental vocabulary in context

## Dialogue: Discussing Environmental Issues

**Context**: Sophie and her friend Thomas are discussing environmental concerns.

---

**Sophie**: Thomas, qu'est-ce que tu penses du réchauffement climatique?
**Thomas**: C'est un problème très sérieux. Je pense que nous devons tous faire notre part.

**Sophie**: Oui, je suis d'accord. Moi, je recycle toujours mes déchets.
**Thomas**: C'est bien! Moi, j'essaie de réduire ma consommation de plastique.

**Sophie**: Et pour les transports? Tu prends souvent la voiture?
**Thomas**: Non, j'essaie de prendre les transports en commun ou mon vélo quand c'est possible.

**Sophie**: Moi aussi! Et je marche beaucoup. C'est bon pour l'environnement et pour la santé.
**Thomas**: Exactement! Et toi, est-ce que tu manges bio?

**Sophie**: Oui, j'essaie d'acheter des produits locaux et bio quand je peux.
**Thomas**: C'est important de soutenir les agriculteurs locaux.

**Sophie**: Et qu'est-ce que tu penses de la pollution de l'air dans les grandes villes?
**Thomas**: C'est un vrai problème. Je pense que les villes devraient investir dans des transports plus propres.

**Sophie**: Je suis d'accord. Et toi, est-ce que tu participes à des actions pour l'environnement?
**Thomas**: Oui, je participe à des nettoyages de plages parfois. Et toi?

**Sophie**: Moi, je fais du bénévolat dans une association qui protège les forêts locales.

## Dialogue: Planning an Eco-Friendly Trip

**Context**: Marie and Paul are planning a vacation and discussing eco-friendly options.

---

**Marie**: Paul, nous devons planifier nos vacances. Où est-ce que tu veux aller?
**Paul**: Je ne sais pas encore. Mais j'aimerais que ce soit un voyage respectueux de l'environnement.

**Marie**: Bonne idée! Et si nous allions dans un parc national?
**Paul**: Oui, j'adorerais ça! Nous pourrions faire de la randonnée et observer la faune et la flore.

**Marie**: Et pour le transport?
**Paul**: Nous pourrions prendre le train au lieu de l'avion. C'est plus écologique.

**Marie**: D'accord. Et pour l'hébergement?
**Paul**: Il y a des écolodges dans certains parcs nationaux. Ce serait parfait!

**Marie**: Et nous devrions apporter nos propres bouteilles d'eau pour éviter le plastique.
**Paul**: Exactement! Et nous pourrions aussi emporter des sacs réutilisables pour nos achats.

**Marie**: Ce sera des vacances formidables et écologiques!
**Paul**: Oui, et nous pourrons en apprendre plus sur la protection de l'environnement.

## Grammar: Future Tense

### Simple Future (Futur Simple)

The **simple future** is used to express **actions that will happen** in the future. For most verbs, it's formed by adding the **future endings** to the **infinitive stem**.

**Future Endings:**
| Subject | Ending | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| je | -ai | /ay/ |
| tu | -as | /a/ |
| il/elle/on | -a | /a/ |
| nous | -ons | /on/ |
| vous | -ez | /ay/ |
| ils/elles | -ont | /on/ |

**Regular Verbs:**
- **Parler** (to speak): je parler**ai**, tu parler**as**, il parler**a**, nous parler**ons**, vous parler**ez**, ils parler**ont**
- **Finir** (to finish): je finir**ai**, tu finir**as**, il finir**a**, nous finir**ons**, vous finir**ez**, ils finir**ont**
- **Vendre** (to sell): je vendr**ai**, tu vendr**as**, il vendr**a**, nous vendr**ons**, vous vendr**ez**, ils vendr**ont**

**Examples:**
- Je **mangerai** une pizza. (I will eat a pizza.)
- Tu **visiteras** le musée. (You will visit the museum.)
- Il **protegera** l'environnement. (He will protect the environment.)
- Nous **recyclerons** nos déchets. (We will recycle our waste.)
- Vous **planterez** des arbres. (You will plant trees.)
- Elles **réduiront** leur consommation. (They will reduce their consumption.)

**Irregular Stems:**
Some verbs have **irregular stems** in the future tense:

| Infinitive | Stem | Example (je form) |
|-----------|------|-------------------|
| aller | ir- | j'irai (I will go) |
| avoir | aur- | j'aurai (I will have) |
| être | ser- | je serai (I will be) |
| faire | fer- | je ferai (I will do/make) |
| pouvoir | pourr- | je pourrai (I will be able to) |
| vouloir | voudr- | je voudrai (I will want) |
| savoir | saur- | je saurai (I will know) |
| devoir | devr- | je devrai (I will have to) |
| venir | viendr- | je viendrai (I will come) |
| tenir | tiendr- | je tiendrai (I will hold) |
| envoyer | enverr- | j'enverrai (I will send) |

**Examples with Irregular Verbs:**
- Je **serai** là. (I will be there.)
- Tu **auras** raison. (You will be right.)
- Il **ira** au parc. (He will go to the park.)
- Nous **ferons** de notre mieux. (We will do our best.)
- Vous **pourrez** réussir. (You will be able to succeed.)
- Ils **voudront** venir. (They will want to come.)

### Near Future (Futur Proche)

The **near future** is formed with **aller + infinitive** and is used for **actions that will happen soon** or **in the near future**.

**Formation:**
- je vais + infinitive
- tu vas + infinitive
- il/elle/on va + infinitive
- nous allons + infinitive
- vous allez + infinitive
- ils/elles vont + infinitive

**Examples:**
- Je **vais recycler** mes déchets. (I am going to recycle my waste.)
- Tu **vas planter** un arbre. (You are going to plant a tree.)
- Il **va protéger** l'environnement. (He is going to protect the environment.)
- Nous **allons économiser** l'énergie. (We are going to save energy.)
- Vous **allez participer** à une action. (You are going to participate in an action.)
- Elles **vont nettoyer** la plage. (They are going to clean the beach.)

**Near Future vs. Simple Future:**
- **Near Future**: Actions happening **soon** or **in the immediate future**
  - Je **vais partir** demain. (I am going to leave tomorrow.)
- **Simple Future**: Actions happening **later** or **in the more distant future**
  - Je **partirai** l'année prochaine. (I will leave next year.)

### Conditional Mood (Conditionnel)

The **conditional mood** is used to express **hypothetical situations**, **polite requests**, or **actions that would happen under certain conditions**.

**Formation:**
The conditional is formed by using the **future stem** (same as futur simple) and adding the **conditional endings**.

**Conditional Endings:**
| Subject | Ending | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| je | -ais | /ay/ |
| tu | -ais | /ay/ |
| il/elle/on | -ait | /ay/ |
| nous | -ions | /yon/ |
| vous | -iez | /yeh/ |
| ils/elles | -aient | /ay/ |

**Regular Verbs:**
- **Parler**: je parler**ais**, tu parler**ais**, il parler**ait**, nous parler**ions**, vous parler**iez**, ils parler**aient**
- **Finir**: je finir**ais**, tu finir**ais**, il finir**ait**, nous finir**ions**, vous finir**iez**, ils finir**aient**

**Examples:**
- Je **recyclerais** si j'avais le temps. (I would recycle if I had time.)
- Tu **protegerais** l'environnement si tu pouvais. (You would protect the environment if you could.)
- Il **planterait** un arbre s'il avait de l'espace. (He would plant a tree if he had space.)
- Nous **réduirions** nos déchets si c'était possible. (We would reduce our waste if it were possible.)

**Irregular Stems (same as future):**
- **Aller**: j'ir**ais** (I would go)
- **Avoir**: j'aur**ais** (I would have)
- **Être**: je ser**ais** (I would be)
- **Faire**: je fer**ais** (I would do)
- **Pouvoir**: je pourr**ais** (I would be able to)
- **Vouloir**: je voudr**ais** (I would want)
- **Savoir**: je saur**ais** (I would know)
- **Devoir**: je devr**ais** (I would have to)

**Examples with Irregular Verbs:**
- Je **serais** plus écologique si j'avais plus d'argent. (I would be more eco-friendly if I had more money.)
- Tu **aurais** moins de déchets si tu recyclais. (You would have less waste if you recycled.)
- Il **irait** en vélo s'il avait un vélo. (He would go by bike if he had a bike.)
- Nous **ferions** plus pour l'environnement si nous avions plus de temps. (We would do more for the environment if we had more time.)

**Polite Requests with Conditional:**
- **Je voudrais** un café, s'il vous plaît. (I would like a coffee, please.)
- **Pourriez-vous** m'aider? (Could you help me?)
- **Auriez-vous** des informations? (Would you have some information?)

**Hypothetical Situations:**
- Si j'avais plus d'argent, je **voyagerais** plus. (If I had more money, I would travel more.)
- Si tout le monde recyclait, la planète **serait** plus propre. (If everyone recycled, the planet would be cleaner.)
- Si nous réduisions notre consommation, nous **économiserions** de l'énergie. (If we reduced our consumption, we would save energy.)

### Conditional Perfect

The **conditional perfect** is used to express **what would have happened** under certain conditions in the past.

**Formation:**
- **auxiliary verb (avoir or être) in conditional** + **past participle**

**Examples:**
- J'**aurais recyclé** si j'avais su. (I would have recycled if I had known.)
- Tu **serais allé(e)** au parc si tu avais eu le temps. (You would have gone to the park if you had had time.)
- Il **aurait protégé** l'environnement s'il avait pu. (He would have protected the environment if he had been able to.)
- Nous **aurions économisé** l'énergie si nous avions pensé à ça. (We would have saved energy if we had thought about it.)

## Cultural Note: Environmental Awareness in France

France has a **strong environmental consciousness** and has been at the **forefront of environmental protection** in Europe. Here's an overview of environmental awareness and actions in France:

### France's Environmental Policies:

- **Paris Agreement**: France was a **key player** in the **2015 Paris Agreement** on climate change, hosting the **COP21 conference** that led to the historic accord.
- **Energy Transition Law**: The **2015 Energy Transition for Green Growth Law** aims to **reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 40%** by 2030 and **increase renewable energy to 32%** of final energy consumption.
- **Nuclear Energy**: France **gets about 70% of its electricity from nuclear power**, the highest percentage in the world. This **low-carbon energy source** helps reduce CO2 emissions.
- **Carbon Tax**: France has implemented a **carbon tax** on fossil fuels to **encourage the transition to cleaner energy sources**.
- **Plastic Ban**: France was one of the first countries to **ban single-use plastic bags** (2016) and has since extended bans to other **single-use plastic items**.
- **Pesticide Reduction**: France aims to **reduce pesticide use by 50%** by 2025 through its **Ecophyto plan**.

### French Environmental Organizations:

- **Greenpeace France**: The French branch of the **international environmental organization**, active in campaigns against **nuclear power, climate change, and pollution**.
- **WWF France**: The French branch of the **World Wide Fund for Nature**, working on **wildlife conservation, climate change, and sustainable development**.
- **Les Amis de la Terre**: **Friends of the Earth France**, focusing on **environmental justice, climate change, and corporate accountability**.
- **Réseau Action Climat**: The **French network of NGOs** working on **climate change issues**.
- **Fondation Nicolas Hulot**: An environmental foundation **promoting ecological transition** and **raising awareness** about environmental issues.
- **Surfrider Foundation Europe**: An organization **dedicated to the protection of oceans and coastlines**, with a strong presence in France.

### French Environmental Initiatives:

- **"Grenelle de l'Environnement"**: A **2007-2008** series of meetings that led to **major environmental policy changes** in France.
- **"Conférence Environnementale"**: An annual **environmental conference** that brings together **government, NGOs, and citizens** to discuss environmental issues.
- **"Semaine du Développement Durable"**: The **Sustainable Development Week**, an annual event **promoting sustainable practices**.
- **"Fête de la Nature"**: The **Nature Festival**, celebrating **biodiversity and nature conservation**.
- **"Journées du Patrimoine"**: The **Heritage Days**, which include **environmental and cultural heritage sites**.

### Waste Management in France:

- **Recycling**: France has a **well-developed recycling system** with **color-coded bins** for different types of waste:
  - **Yellow bin** (poubelle jaune): Plastic, metal, cartons, paper
  - **Green bin** (poubelle verte): Glass
  - **Blue bin** (poubelle bleue): Paper (in some areas)
  - **Brown bin** (poubelle marron): Organic waste (in some areas)
  - **Regular bin** (poubelle normale): Non-recyclable waste
- **Recycling Rate**: France **recycles about 47% of its household waste**, with a target of **65% by 2025**.
- **Composting**: **Composting is encouraged**, and many cities provide **compost bins** for organic waste.
- **Waste Reduction**: The **"Zéro Déchet"** (Zero Waste) movement is **gaining popularity** in France.

### Renewable Energy in France:

- **Hydropower**: The **largest source of renewable energy** in France, accounting for about **10% of electricity production**.
- **Wind Power**: **Wind energy is growing rapidly**, with France being the **5th largest wind power producer** in the world.
- **Solar Power**: **Solar energy production is increasing**, especially in **southern France**.
- **Bioenergy**: France produces **bioenergy from various sources**, including **wood, biogas, and biofuels**.
- **Geothermal Energy**: Used in **some regions**, particularly for **heating**.

**Targets**: France aims to **increase renewable energy to 32% of final energy consumption by 2030** and **40% by 2050**.

### Sustainable Transportation in France:

- **Public Transportation**: France has an **extensive public transportation network**, including **metro, buses, trams, and trains**.
- **High-Speed Rail**: The **TGV (Train à Grande Vitesse)** is a **high-speed train** that connects major French cities at speeds up to **320 km/h**.
- **Electric Vehicles**: France is **promoting electric vehicles** through **subsidies and incentives**.
- **Bike Sharing**: **Vélib'** in Paris and other cities offers **bike-sharing programs**.
- **Car Sharing**: **Autolib'** (now replaced by other services) was a **pioneering electric car-sharing program**.
- **Carpooling**: **BlaBlaCar** is a **popular carpooling app** that connects drivers with empty seats to passengers.

**Targets**: France aims to **end the sale of gasoline and diesel vehicles by 2040**.

### French Environmental Education:

- **School Programs**: Environmental education is **integrated into the school curriculum** at all levels.
- **"Éco-École"**: A program that **encourages schools to adopt eco-friendly practices**.
- **"Éco-Délégués"**: **Student eco-representatives** in schools who **promote environmental awareness**.
- **University Programs**: Many French universities offer **environmental science and sustainability programs**.
- **Research Institutes**: France has **several research institutes** dedicated to **environmental studies**, such as the **CNRS (National Center for Scientific Research)**.

### French Environmental Challenges:

- **Air Pollution**: **Air pollution is a major issue** in French cities, particularly **Paris, Lyon, and Marseille**.
- **Water Pollution**: **Water quality is a concern**, particularly in **agricultural areas** where **pesticides and fertilizers** can contaminate water sources.
- **Waste Management**: While France has a **good recycling system**, there are still **challenges with waste reduction and illegal dumping**.
- **Biodiversity Loss**: France has **lost some of its biodiversity**, particularly due to **agricultural intensification and urbanization**.
- **Climate Change**: France is **vulnerable to climate change impacts**, including **rising temperatures, changing rainfall patterns, and sea-level rise**.

### French Environmental Success Stories:

- **Ozone Layer Protection**: France was **one of the first countries to ratify the Montreal Protocol** on substances that deplete the ozone layer.
- **Renewable Energy Growth**: France has **significantly increased its renewable energy production** in recent years.
- **Forest Protection**: France has **increased its forest cover** and has **strict laws protecting forests**.
- **Water Quality**: France has **improved water quality** in its rivers and lakes through **better wastewater treatment**.
- **Biodiversity Conservation**: France has **established numerous protected areas**, including **national parks and nature reserves**.

### French Environmental Innovations:

- **Solar Road**: France has **experimented with solar roads** (routes solaires) that **generate electricity from sunlight**.
- **Vertical Forests**: **Vertical forest buildings** are being constructed in French cities to **increase green spaces**.
- **Smart Grids**: France is **developing smart grid technology** to **improve energy efficiency**.
- **Green Roofs**: **Green roofs** (toits végétalisés) are becoming **more common** in French cities.
- **Electric Aviation**: France is **investing in electric aviation** to **reduce emissions from air travel**.

### French Environmental Figures:

- **Nicolas Hulot**: A **famous environmental activist, journalist, and former Minister of Ecological Transition**.
- **Yann Arthus-Bertrand**: A **photographer, journalist, and environmental activist** known for his **aerial photography and documentaries**.
- **Corinne Lepage**: A **politician and environmental lawyer** who has **worked on environmental legislation**.
- **Pierre Rabhi**: A **farmer, writer, and environmental thinker** who **promoted agroecology and sustainable agriculture**.
- **Cyril Dion**: A **writer, poet, and environmental activist** who **co-founded the Colibris movement**.

### French Environmental Festivals and Events:

- **"Fête de la Nature"**: Celebrated in **May**, this festival **raises awareness about nature conservation**.
- **"Semaine du Développement Durable"**: Held in **June**, this week **promotes sustainable development practices**.
- **"Journées Européennes du Patrimoine"**: Held in **September**, these days **celebrate cultural and natural heritage**.
- **"Festival du Film Environnemental"**: A **film festival dedicated to environmental themes**.
- **"Assises Nationales de la Biodiversité"**: A **national conference on biodiversity** held every two years.

### Environmental Tourism in France:

- **Ecotourism**: France is a **leader in ecotourism**, with **numerous eco-lodges, nature reserves, and sustainable tourism initiatives**.
- **National Parks**: France has **11 national parks** and **54 regional nature parks** that **protect diverse ecosystems**.
- **Green Key Label**: The **"Clef Verte"** (Green Key) is a **label for eco-friendly accommodations** in France.
- **Greenways**: France has **developed greenways** (voies vertes) for **walking and cycling** in natural areas.
- **Whale Watching**: In **Brittany and the Mediterranean**, whale watching tours are **popular eco-tourism activities**.

### Tips for Eco-Friendly Travel in France:

- **Use Public Transportation**: France has an **excellent public transportation system** that's **eco-friendly**.
- **Choose Eco-Lodges**: Look for **"Clef Verte"** (Green Key) labeled accommodations.
- **Eat Local**: **Support local farmers and producers** by eating at **farm-to-table restaurants** and buying at **farmers' markets**.
- **Reduce Waste**: **Bring reusable bags, bottles, and containers** to **minimize waste**.
- **Respect Nature**: **Follow marked trails**, **don't pick plants**, and **don't disturb wildlife**.
- **Use Bike Sharing**: Many French cities have **bike-sharing programs** that are **great for eco-friendly exploration**.
- **Support Eco-Tourism**: **Choose eco-friendly tour operators** that **respect the environment**.

### French Environmental Proverbs:

- **"La Terre n'est pas un don de nos parents, mais un emprunt à nos enfants."** - "The Earth is not a gift from our parents, but a loan from our children."
- **"On n'hérite pas de la Terre de nos ancêtres, on l'emprunte à nos enfants."** - "We do not inherit the Earth from our ancestors; we borrow it from our children." (Native American proverb, popular in France)
- **"Petites causes, grands effets."** - "Small causes, big effects."
- **"Qui peut le plus, peut le moins."** - "He who can do more, can do less." (Encouraging doing what you can for the environment)
- **"Un pour tous, tous pour un."** - "One for all, all for one." (Encouraging collective action)
