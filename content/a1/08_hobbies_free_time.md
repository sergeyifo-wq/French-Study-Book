# A1 Unit 08: Hobbies and Free Time

## Learning Objectives
- Learn vocabulary for hobbies and free time activities
- Talk about what you like to do in your free time
- Use the verb "faire" (to do/make)
- Use the verb "jouer" (to play)
- Ask and answer questions about hobbies
- Describe frequency (how often you do activities)

## Dialogue: Talking About Hobbies

**Context**: Sophie and Marie are talking about their free time activities.

---

**Sophie**: Marie, qu'est-ce que tu aimes faire pendant ton temps libre?
**Marie**: J'aime beaucoup lire. Et toi, Sophie?

**Sophie**: Moi, j'adore faire du sport. Je fais du yoga et de la natation.
**Marie**: C'est super! Moi, je ne fais pas beaucoup de sport, mais j'aime marcher.

**Sophie**: Tu aimes lire quoi?
**Marie**: J'aime lire des romans et des magazines. Et toi, tu lis?

**Sophie**: Oui, parfois. Mais je préfère regarder des films. J'adore le cinéma!
**Marie**: Moi aussi! Quel est ton film préféré?

**Sophie**: J'adore les films français. Et toi?
**Marie**: Moi, je préfère les comédies. Elles me font rire.

**Sophie**: Tu fais autre chose pendant ton temps libre?
**Marie**: Oui, j'aime cuisiner et écouter de la musique. Et toi?

**Sophie**: Moi, j'aime voyager et prendre des photos. J'adore la photographie!
**Marie**: C'est passionnant! Tu voyages souvent?

**Sophie**: Oui, quand je peux. Et toi, tu voyages?
**Marie**: Pas souvent, mais j'aimerais visiter plus de pays.

## Grammar: The Verb "Faire" (To Do/Make)

### Present Tense of "Faire"

| Subject | French | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| I | je fais | /zhe fay/ |
| You (singular) | tu fais | /too fay/ |
| He/She/It | il/elle fait | /eel/ell fay/ |
| We | nous faisons | /noo fay-zon/ |
| You (plural) | vous faites | /voo fet/ |
| They | ils/elles font | /eel/ell fon/ |

**Examples:**
- Je fais du sport. (I do sports.)
- Tu fais du yoga? (Do you do yoga?)
- Il fait de la natation. (He does swimming.)
- Nous faisons de la musique. (We make music.)
- Vous faites du vélo? (Do you do cycling?)
- Ils font du football. (They play soccer.)

### Important Notes:

1. **With Sports and Activities**: Use "faire du/de la/des" for sports and activities
   - faire **du** football (to play soccer)
   - faire **de la** natation (to do swimming)
   - faire **des** achats (to do shopping)
   - faire **du** vélo (to do cycling)

2. **Common Expressions**:
   - faire du sport (to do sports)
   - faire de la musique (to make music)
   - faire de la peinture (to do painting)
   - faire de la couture (to do sewing)
   - faire du tricot (to do knitting)
   - faire la cuisine (to cook)
   - faire le ménage (to do housework)

3. **Negative Form**: "ne...pas" surrounds the verb
   - Je ne fais pas de sport. (I don't do sports.)
   - Il ne fait pas de musique. (He doesn't make music.)

4. **Question Form**: Invert subject and verb (or use est-ce que)
   - Fais-tu du sport? (Do you do sports?)
   - Est-ce que tu fais de la peinture? (Do you do painting?)

## Grammar: The Verb "Jouer" (To Play)

### Present Tense of "Jouer"

| Subject | French | Pronunciation |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| I | je joue | /zhe zhoo/ |
| You (singular) | tu joues | /too zhoo/ |
| He/She/It | il/elle joue | /eel/ell zhoo/ |
| We | nous jouons | /noo zhoo-on/ |
| You (plural) | vous jouez | /voo zhoo-ay/ |
| They | ils/elles jouent | /eel/ell zhoo/ |

**Examples:**
- Je joue au football. (I play soccer.)
- Tu joues au tennis? (Do you play tennis?)
- Il joue de la guitare. (He plays the guitar.)
- Nous jouons aux cartes. (We play cards.)
- Vous jouez aux échecs? (Do you play chess?)
- Ils jouent à des jeux vidéo. (They play video games.)

### Important Notes:

1. **With Sports**: Use "jouer à" for most sports
   - jouer **à** la balle (to play ball)
   - jouer **à** la cache-cache (to play hide and seek)
   - jouer **au** football (to play soccer)
   - jouer **au** tennis (to play tennis)
   - jouer **au** basketball (to play basketball)

2. **With Musical Instruments**: Use "jouer de"
   - jouer **de** la guitare (to play the guitar)
   - jouer **du** piano (to play the piano)
   - jouer **de** la flute (to play the flute)
   - jouer **du** violon (to play the violin)

3. **With Games**: Use "jouer à"
   - jouer **aux** cartes (to play cards)
   - jouer **aux** échecs (to play chess)
   - jouer **à** des jeux vidéo (to play video games)
   - jouer **à** des jeux de société (to play board games)

4. **Common Expressions**:
   - jouer au football (to play soccer)
   - jouer au tennis (to play tennis)
   - jouer de la guitare (to play the guitar)
   - jouer du piano (to play the piano)
   - jouer aux cartes (to play cards)

## Cultural Note: Leisure Time in France

Free time and hobbies are important aspects of French culture. Here are some insights into how the French spend their leisure time:

**Work-Life Balance**: The French value their free time and have a strong work-life balance. The legal work week is 35 hours, and most people have at least 5 weeks of paid vacation per year.

**"Les Loisirs"**: The French word for hobbies is "les loisirs" (leisure activities). Common loisirs include:

**Sports**: Sports are very popular in France:
- **Football (soccer)**: The most popular sport, with a strong national team and professional league
- **Rugby**: Especially popular in the south of France
- **Tennis**: France hosts the French Open (Roland Garros), one of the four Grand Slam tournaments
- **Cycling**: Very popular, both as a sport and for transportation
- **Pétanque**: A traditional French game similar to bocce, often played in parks

**Cultural Activities**: The French have a rich cultural life:
- **Cinema**: The French love going to the cinema. France has a strong film industry and hosts the Cannes Film Festival.
- **Theater**: Theater is popular, especially in cities like Paris.
- **Museums**: France has many world-class museums, with the Louvre being the most famous.
- **Music**: The French enjoy various types of music, from classical to modern pop.
- **Festivals**: There are many festivals throughout France, celebrating music, film, food, and more.

**Reading**: The French are avid readers:
- France has a strong literary tradition with famous authors like Victor Hugo, Marcel Proust, and Albert Camus.
- Bookstores (librairies) and libraries (bibliothèques) are common in cities and towns.
- Many people read newspapers and magazines regularly.

**Food and Cooking**: Cooking and food are important hobbies:
- Many French people enjoy cooking at home.
- Cooking classes are popular.
- Food markets (marchés) are social gathering places.
- Food blogs and cooking shows are very popular.

**Outdoor Activities**: The French enjoy spending time outdoors:
- **Pique-nique**: Picnics are very popular, especially in parks and by the water.
- **Randonnée**: Hiking is a popular way to enjoy nature.
- **Jardinage**: Gardening is a common hobby, especially among older people.
- **Balades**: Taking walks or strolls is a favorite pastime.

**Socializing**: The French enjoy spending time with friends and family:
- **Apéritif**: A pre-dinner drink with snacks, often with friends
- **Dîner**: Dinner parties at home are common
- **Cafés**: Meeting at cafés is a popular social activity
- **Associations**: Many French people belong to clubs or associations based on their hobbies

**Holidays and Vacations**: The French take their vacations seriously:
- Most people take several weeks of vacation per year.
- August is a popular month for vacations, with many businesses closing.
- Popular vacation destinations include the beach, the mountains, and the countryside.
- Many French people have second homes (résidences secondaires) in vacation areas.

**"La Dolce Vita"**: The French approach to leisure is often relaxed and enjoyable. They value taking time to enjoy life's pleasures, whether it's a good meal, a walk in the park, or time with friends and family.
